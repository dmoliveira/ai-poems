# Verso roadmap

## Launch: a useful first shelf

The launch has 161 sourced works, eight original languages, static poem pages, search and filtering, a local shelf, multilingual reader controls, basic sourced connections, and Pages deployment. Coverage is uneven: 154 entries are Shakespeare's sonnets, most translations are not yet available, and some contextual records need further editorial research.

## Grow toward 1,000+ works

Expand in reviewed batches by language, era, author and geography. Prioritize diverse public-domain or permission-cleared editions, evidence for dates and textual variants, and separate rights checks for each translation. Add documented influence and response edges with sources, and replace unreviewed reading aids with attributed, rights-cleared or independently reviewed translations. Grow the catalogue based on editorial quality rather than generating placeholder entries.

## Enrich the experience

Consider dedicated author profiles, more curated collections, accessible interface localization, better scalable indexing and pagination, a timeline and map, import/export of the local shelf, and optional audio where rights permit. These are future enhancements, not features claimed for the MVP.
