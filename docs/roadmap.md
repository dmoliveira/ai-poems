# Verso roadmap

## Launch: a useful first shelf

The launch introduced 161 sourced works, eight original languages, static poem pages, search and filtering, a local shelf, multilingual reader controls, basic sourced connections, and Pages deployment.

## Beyond 1,000 works

The catalogue now contains more than 2,200 works from over 200 named or traditional author groups across all eight original languages. Coverage remains uneven across poets, languages and periods, and translations are much rarer than originals. Continue expanding and independently checking batches by language, era, author and geography. Prioritize diverse public-domain or permission-cleared editions, evidence for dates and textual variants, and separate rights checks for each translation. Add documented influence and response edges with sources, and keep AI-assisted reading aids clearly labeled while expanding independently reviewed alternatives. Grow the catalogue based on editorial quality rather than generating placeholder entries.

## Enrich the experience

Shareable poet and traditional-voice pages now collect works using existing catalogue metadata; they do not invent biographies. Consider more curated collections, accessible interface localization, better scalable indexing and pagination, a timeline and map, import/export of the local shelf, and optional audio where rights permit.
